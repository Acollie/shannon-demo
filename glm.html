GLM v0: ask the movie graph, watch it walk

Shannon reads your question with a small frozen encoder, turns it into a chain of relations, and walks a knowledge graph to the answer. Everything runs on this device: the encoder, the scorer and the graph. No server sees your question.

About this demo

GLM ("graph language model") is Shannon plus a knowledge graph. The language part is small and frozen; the facts live in the graph, which loads and unloads as a pack. Unloading the movies pack really removes those facts: ask again and it has nothing to say.

How an answer is made
1. The linker finds the longest entity name from the loaded packs in your question (it uses the pack's own entity list, the names in the dataset's graph) and replaces it with a placeholder. 2. MiniLM gives one vector per word piece. A linear tagger turns each into a soft vote over relations, and the votes sum into a 16×16 question operator. 3. Every relation path of 1 to 3 steps that exists from that entity is scored by cosine between the question operator and the product of the relations' 16×16 operators. 4. The best path that reaches something is walked; answers are ranked by how many paths reach them.
Tiny
A 9.6k (MetaQA) / 0.2M (WebQSP) parameter trained scorer on a frozen 22.7M MiniLM, about 23M parameters in all (27M with the text-graph builder). That is about 1/900 of gpt-oss-20b's parameters and 1/150 of its active parameters.
Energy
About 0.1–0.2 J per question above idle, batched, on an A40, the same as any MiniLM-sized ranker. That is roughly 65–270× less than gpt-oss-20b answering from the same retrieved facts (GPU only for gpt-oss). This page does not measure energy on your device.
How good it is
This scorer (seed 0) was trained on MetaQA 1- and 2-step questions only. On a held-out MetaQA set with the topic entity given it scored 0.97 (1-step), 1.00 (2-step) and 0.78 (3-step) hits@1. MetaQA questions are template phrasings, and the held-out set reuses the training templates, so free-form wording can do worse.
Limits
Movies only for now (WikiMovies, about 2016). One named entity per question; the linker is plain name matching, so an ambiguous or misspelt name can pick the wrong thing. Answers are graph nodes; nothing is generated. Questions outside the nine relations (directed by, written by, starring, year, language, genre, tags, IMDb votes, IMDb rating) get the nearest path anyway. A general Wikidata pack is next.
