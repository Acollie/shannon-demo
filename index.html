A small model that has to think in ideas

Shannon reads up to 128 word-pieces and squeezes them into 32 idea codes, each one of 4,096 entries in a learned codebook. Its decoder then writes the next 128 word-pieces from those codes alone: it never sees your text. Everything below runs on your device with onnxruntime-web; nothing is sent to a server.

your text→frozen MiniLM encoder→32 ideas (codes 0–4095)→idea transformer→decoder→new text

All 4,096 codebook vectors, laid out in 2-D with UMAP (cosine). Nearby codes have similar vectors. Colours are 28 k-means regions, labelled by the words their codes over-represent.
